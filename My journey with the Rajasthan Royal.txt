My journey with the Rajasthan Royals began in 2009, when I was a kid watching IPL matches on a small TV, cheering without fully understanding the game. I had just missed their fairytale title of 2008, but the story had already reached me: a team nobody believed in had beaten the giants. I didn’t choose RR that summer; RR chose me.

Shane Watson taught me what it means to carry a team on your shoulders, bat and ball, game after game. Then came Sanju Samson, a young boy from Kerala who reminded every small-town dreamer that talent finds its stage. Through the near-misses, the rebuilding years and the 2022 final, the pink and blue never faded for me. If anything, it grew deeper.

Seventeen years later I’m still that kid, only now I watch with data as well as heart. This competition feels like a full-circle moment: the team that gave me my love for cricket is now giving me a chance to give something back.

Halla Bol!
